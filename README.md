# RH VISUALS – Business Management System

An AI-enabled business management system designed to streamline portfolio management, client information, event bookings, and business operations for RH VISUALS.

## Features

- Portfolio and Service Management
- Client and Event Booking Management
- AI-Powered Quotation Generator
- AI Chatbot for Customer Assistance
- Business Analytics Dashboard
- Multi-Admin Support with Role-Based Access Control
- Digital Asset Management

## Technology Stack

- Frontend: React.js
- Backend: Node.js, Express.js
- Database: PostgreSQL
- AI Integration: AI-powered chatbot and quotation generation

## Objective

To simplify business operations, improve client management, automate quotation generation, and provide an efficient digital platform for RH VISUALS.
